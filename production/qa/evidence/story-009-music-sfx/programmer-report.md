## Spec deviations

None. Parameters are the spec's verbatim. Three implementation reconciliations the spec left implicit, documented for review:

1. **`musicSetDuck` idle threshold** (`flow ≤ 0.001 → skip`): §2 pins the combat bag entry swell at `setVol(1.0, tau 1.2)`, §3 says duck runs every frame with tau 0.1. Taken literally, frame 1 of combat (flow = 0) would call `setVol(1.0, 0.1)` and cut the swell to ~0.1 s. The threshold makes ducking a no-op when there is nothing to duck (multiplier would be 1.0 anyway) and full per-frame ducking the moment boost moves flow — both spec clauses hold. After a boost release, flow decays asymptotically below the threshold (~1.7 s) and the multiplier rests at ≈0.9996 ≈ 1.
2. **Noise-air layer**: the spec's table lists `loopStart({freq,type:'bandpass',q,playback})` with the layer gain (0.012 / 0.016) separate. I pass the gain as loopStart's `vol` and re-route the inner bag's output node into the music bag's out-gain — identical gain product, and it is what makes crossfades/ducking own the air layer (the spec requires the air to be a layer of the bag). `air.music = true` set right after creation per spec §7.
3. **Idempotency wording**: `musicTo` no-ops only when the target is current **and the bag is alive** (spec's exact clause). A dead bag under a current target rebuilds — this is what self-heals teardown kills (see Finding 1).
